Local-first by design

Your manual stays with you.

DMXtract is built so you can create, correct, test, and export a fixture profile without sending the fixture manual to us.

Manuals and photos

PDFs, screenshots, and photos are read in your browser. DMXtract does not upload them to a hosted application service, AI service, or analytics provider.

Projects saved in your browser

Autosave stores the editable fixture project—not the original manual—in this browser's local storage. You can remove it by clearing site data for dmxtract.sho.run. A downloaded .dmxtract.json file stays wherever you save it.

Optional fixture lookup

If the site operator enables existing-profile lookup, DMXtract may send the detected manufacturer, model, and mode sizes to its same-origin lookup service. It does not send the manual, filename, channel table, bridge token, DMX network details, or hardware identity.

Testing a physical light

The optional DMXtract Bridge listens only on your own computer. Browser pairing is bound to the requesting website origin, expires when the bridge quits, and starts output at zero. Fixture output is not routed through a hosted service.

Normal hosting data

Like any website, the hosting provider necessarily processes ordinary request information such as your IP address to deliver files. DMXtract has no account requirement, advertising tracker, behavioral analytics, or document-upload endpoint.

Links to other services

If you choose a result from GDTF Share or follow a GitHub link, that service's privacy terms apply after you leave DMXtract.

Questions or corrections

Open an issue in the DMXtract GitHub project. Please do not attach a manual unless you have permission to redistribute it.
