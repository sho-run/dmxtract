# Beginner usability gate

Recruit at least five people who are students, volunteers, amateur DJs, or first-time lighting-software users. Give each person a simple RGB/RGBW fixture, its manual, and a laptop with no walkthrough.

The release gate is:

- Four of five finish without outside explanation.
- Median completion time is under five minutes.
- Participants can say where their manual data went and understand that it stayed local.
- Participants can find and repair an intentionally incorrect channel range.
- Participants recover by selecting a table region when automatic extraction fails.
- Every required screen has one visually dominant next action and no unexplained acronym.
- Keyboard-only completion, 44 px touch targets, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and reduced motion pass.

Record completion time, recovery point, wrong turns, terms that required explanation, and whether the user noticed the Stop output control. Do not record or upload the participant's manual without separate consent.
